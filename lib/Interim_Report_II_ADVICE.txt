* Abstract: A concise summary of your work, outlining the broad aims and actual achievements of the project. Normally, there will be emphasis on the significant aspects that contribute to the value of the project. This summary must be able to be read in isolation from the rest of the report, and thus should be complete within itself. The abstract should normally be around one to two pages.
